In Australia he works with Goodspace (Kingsgrove, Rouse Hill, Eastgardens) and Be A Little. His edge is joining media buying, clean data and automation: GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta CAPI and cross-domain tracking, plus marketing automation and CRM systems he builds for brands in n8n, GoHighLevel and Zapier. He holds 9 Google certifications and studied computer engineering.
